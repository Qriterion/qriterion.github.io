How to read these cards

  • Benchmark means a stated workload and protocol produces a result. Compare only identical protocols.
  • Vendor metric and capability descriptor are retained, but are not universal ranking scores.
  • Reported Benchmark is vendor-provided data. Validated Benchmark is a result QCVV has independently verified.
  • Comparator keeps source language such as >; it is not silently changed into an exact value.
