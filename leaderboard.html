How to read the leaderboard

  • Position, not bar height alone, indicates the ranking: the rightmost QPU is most favourable under the stated direction.
  • Only QPUs with a directly measured value for the selected metric are included.
  • Reported and Validated Benchmarks are separate evidence sets and are never compared on one chart. Validated results are standardized physical-hardware records, not Qriterion reruns.
